Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Who this covers

GT Uh Oh is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @gtuhoh. It does not collect information from anyone else, and it has no visitors' accounts, cookies, analytics, or advertising.

TikTok data

When the owner connects @gtuhoh, TikTok provides an access token and basic profile information (such as the display name and avatar) for that account only. These are used solely to publish the owner's approved clips and to show posting status. They are stored on the owner's own computer, are never sold or shared, and can be removed at any time by disconnecting the app in TikTok's settings.

Gameplay recordings

Recordings and clips stay on the owner's own computer until the owner chooses to post one.
